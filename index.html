BNO
U.S. Brent Oil Fund
Energy/Commodity

The United States Brent Oil Fund (BNO) tracks the daily price movements of Brent crude oil. Unlike WTI, Brent is the global benchmark. Because roughly 20% of global oil transits the Strait of Hormuz, Brent is uniquely sensitive to Middle Eastern geopolitical shocks. It holds a massive "fear premium" during maritime conflicts.

Direct proxy for global crude. Immediate beneficiary of Hormuz blockade threat.
